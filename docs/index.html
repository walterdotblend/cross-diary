EVERYTHING YOU NEED

Handcrafted Capabilities for Mindful Writing

🏝️

Dynamic Island HUD

A permanent interactive command pill docked at the top screen edge on Windows and Android. Access playback, scratchpad, and timer instantly.

🎮

Smart Game Mode

Seamlessly retracts into a discreet 1px edge guideline when fullscreen video games or borderless apps launch, never blocking your crosshair or FPS.

🧩

Modular NoteBlocks

Embed interactive checklists, Pomodoro clocks, and word counters right inside your sheets with exact line-by-line keyboard and button controls.

☁️

Real-Time Cloud Sync

Write on your desktop machine and watch it appear instantaneously on your phone. Clean encrypted vaults with automatic 30-day inactivity protection.

📋

Universal Clipboard

Copy snippets or links on PC and tap to paste directly from your Android push notification buttons, labelled with custom device names.

🔒

Encrypted Vaults

Password-protected diary partitions with client-side encryption. Keep private personal thoughts protected offline and over the wire.

🎨

AI Prompts & Custom CSS

Skin your journal with Neon Brutalism, Kawaii Glow or Borderless Tiling. Click the AI Prompt button to craft bespoke CSS with any LLM.

🎵

Pulse Audio

Integrated lo-fi audio player and ambient generator. Listen to rain, cafe sounds or your voice recordings right from the top notch.
